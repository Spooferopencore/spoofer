Troubleshooting "Could Not Sign In to iMessage"

  • Unregistered Serial Number: Check the serial on Apple Coverage. An ideal hackintosh serial shows "Purchase Date Not Validated" or "We're unable to check coverage", meaning Apple's database recognizes the model format without assigning it to an existing physical user.
  • Built-in Ethernet (en0): Run ifconfig. If your ethernet is en1 or en2, use NullEthernet.kext or adjust your ACPI device properties.
  • Native NVRAM: OpenCore must be able to read and write to NVRAM. If your motherboard has broken NVRAM, enable EmulateNVRAM.kext or configure OpenRuntime quirks.

OpenCore EFI Identity Installation Guide

5-step verified workflow for injecting SMBIOS into your OpenCore bootloader partition.

1

Mount Your System EFI Partition

On macOS, open Terminal and mount your primary EFI partition using diskutil:

sudo diskutil mount disk0s1

On Windows, run an elevated Administrator command prompt and use mountvol B: /S to mount the system partition to drive letter B:.

2

Locate and Open config.plist

Navigate to your OpenCore root directory:

/Volumes/EFI/EFI/OC/config.plist

Make a backup of your original working file: cp config.plist config.plist.backup

3

Update PlatformInfo -> Generic

Use the Config.plist Injector tab above to inject the new keys, or manually set:

• SystemProductName
• SystemSerialNumber
• MLB
• SystemUUID
• ROM (<data> Base64)

Ensure Automatic = true and UpdateSMBIOS = true.

4

Perform a Clean NVRAM Reset

Reboot your machine. In the OpenCore Boot Picker menu, press spacebar to reveal hidden auxiliary tools, and select Reset NVRAM. This clears old cached board serial numbers from motherboards and firmware.

5

Verify in macOS System Information

Boot into macOS, click the Apple logo → About This Mac, and verify that the Model Name, Serial Number, and Hardware UUID match your newly generated profile.

OEM Laptop Quirks Notice (Dell / Lenovo / HP)

Many Dell, Lenovo, and HP laptops require PlatformInfo -> UpdateSMBIOSMode = Custom combined with Kernel -> Quirks -> CustomSMBIOSGuid = true. This prevents the BIOS ACPI tables from clashing with Windows OEM license keys when dual-booting.
